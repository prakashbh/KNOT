A connected body of work 2022–2026

At a glance

This portfolio brings together 19 selected publications produced between 2022 and 2026. The work includes journal articles, conference papers, and a book chapter. Across these publications, the recurring interest is how learning environments and problem scenarios can support students as they make sense of ideas, work through problems, and reflect on their learning.

Research contribution map Four connected strands

Questions that run through the portfolio

Learning environments and design

Learning spaces, problem scenarios, scaffolding, case-study design, and the use of learning outcomes to shape educational experiences.

Computational thinking within PBL

Abstraction, decomposition, pattern recognition, and related approaches to structuring inquiry and problem work.

Knowledge construction

Knowledge structuring and construction, alongside studies of computational-thinking-oriented learning activities.

Assessment and pedagogical extensions

Portfolios, reflection, simulations, game-based learning, AI-assisted instructional design, and activity-based assessment, including Techno-Fun Week.

These strands are related, but the publications address different questions and settings. Together, they document a developing programme of work around the design, use, and study of learning activities. They should be read as connected studies, not as repeated demonstrations of one result.
