The Research Journey

This work ran from 2022 to 2025 and began with a teacher’s question about making each class more curious. It follows three research stages, each with a different student batch: initial classroom exploration, the pilot study, and the official study, before arriving at the final KNOT framework.

The route through the research

How the research was built

The work followed a connected sequence: define the learning question, learn from early classroom experience, design and examine the main study, interpret what the evidence showed, then shape a framework teachers can use. Each phase informed the next.

  1. Phase 1 · Set the foundationChapter 1Introduction

    Frame knowledge construction and the learner capacities the study explores.

  2. Chapter 2Background Study

    Bring together PBL, computational thinking, and learning theory.

  3. Chapter 3Gap Analysis

    Identify what the study needs to understand and address.

  4. Chapter 4Course Description

    Set out the course context and learning experiences.

  5. Phase 2 · Learn from practiceChapter 5Initial Exploration

    Use early course feedback to surface questions for further study.

  6. Chapter 6Pilot Study

    Examine an initial PBL experience and what it revealed.

  7. Chapter 7KNOT Framework Version 0

    Record the first framework shaped by the pilot.

  8. Phase 3 · Plan and gather evidenceChapter 8Research Design

    Define the questions, evidence sources, and analysis plan.

  9. Chapter 9Course Delivery and Quantitative Evidence

    Present the course design, student responses, project evidence, and scores.

  10. Chapter 10Qualitative Findings

    Explore student accounts of initiative, problem solving, uncertainty, and transfer.

  11. Phase 4 · Interpret and connectChapter 11Discussion

    Connect findings back to each research question.

  12. Chapter 12Emergence of KNOT

    Synthesize what the study reveals about the framework.

  13. Phase 5 · Share the contributionChapter 13The KNOT’s Philosophy

    State the view of learning developed through the research.

  14. Chapter 14KNOT Framework

    Translate the learning into four moves teachers can use.

  15. Chapter 15Conclusion

    Answer the central question and set out future scope.
